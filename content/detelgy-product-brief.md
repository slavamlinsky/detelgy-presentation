# Detelgy — Product & Marketing Brief

**Document type:** Source of truth for stakeholder storytelling and presentation production  
**Version:** Draft 1.0 — October 15, 2026  
**Audience:** Founders, stakeholders, prospective partners, presentation designers and Codex  
**Status:** Strategic working draft. Product availability and strategic assumptions must be revalidated before external publication.

## 1. Executive summary

**Detelgy is a global professional growth platform for polygraph examiners.** It is designed to help professionals strengthen their expertise, grow professional connections and recognition, and unlock new earning opportunities.

Detelgy brings together a professional community, discoverable expert profiles, a marketplace for client services, peer consultations and mentorship, publications, and eventually structured training and education.

**Core promise:** Help polygraph professionals become more successful—not simply use another professional website.

**Strategic tagline (proposed):** *Where expertise becomes opportunity.*

**Positioning (proposed):** *The global professional ecosystem for polygraph examiners.*

## 2. Why Detelgy exists

A capable examiner's professional outcomes are not determined by technical skill alone. Visibility, trusted relationships, access to clients, continual learning, and the ability to demonstrate expertise also matter.

The working hypothesis is that these activities are fragmented across personal networks, messaging groups, social platforms, individual websites, directories, professional associations, training providers, and local referrals. Detelgy aims to make them more connected and cumulative.

This is a **product hypothesis**, not a validated claim about every examiner or geography. Expert Discovery interviews should test which pains are urgent enough to drive adoption and payment.

## 3. Mission and vision

**Mission:** Help polygraph professionals develop expertise, build recognized reputations, form meaningful professional connections, and access sustainable income opportunities.

**Vision:** Become the professional home for a globally connected polygraph community—where professional work, learning, contributions, reputation, and opportunities reinforce one another.

**North-star outcomes for users:**
1. **Expertise:** Professionals can learn and solve difficult challenges with peers.
2. **Reputation:** Their professional identity and contributions become more discoverable and credible.
3. **Income opportunity:** They can access additional client demand and monetize expertise through relevant services.

**Enabling mechanism:** Connections and trust, supported by multilingual discovery and communication.

Detelgy **enables opportunities**; it does not guarantee more orders, higher income, or professional standing for each member.

## 4. Whom the platform serves

### Primary supply-side focus

Independent or commercially active polygraph examiners who want to grow their practices and professional standing. Some may work in small firms or combine private and institutional work.

### Other important professional participants

- **Established experts and industry leaders:** Bring experience, credibility, specialized knowledge, peer consultations, and mentorship, even if they no longer conduct many examinations.
- **Emerging professionals:** Seek peer guidance, education, relationships, and a pathway toward building a practice.
- **Training providers and professional partners:** Potential contributors to educational programming, professional events, and future ecosystem expansion.

### Demand-side participants

Individuals, organizations, and business customers searching for suitable polygraph services. Their discovery and booking experience matters to marketplace liquidity, but **this first stakeholder deck is told primarily from the examiner's perspective**.

**Important:** These are strategic segments, not final validated ICPs or research-derived personas. A separate ICP/Personas/JTBD document will define and test them.

## 5. Customer value architecture

| Value pillar | Capabilities | Intended professional outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Connect** | Discussions, answers, professional networking; proposed mastermind sessions and roundtables | Less isolation; useful relationships; peer support |
| **Grow** | Peer learning; proposed consultations, mentorship, and structured Academy/training | Stronger skills, guidance, and professional development |
| **Build Reputation** | Expert profiles, qualifications, reviews, participation, publications; role/recognition badges | Discoverability, credibility, recognition |
| **Earn** | Service listings and booking flow; planned marketplace transactions, paid peer consulting, mentorship, and education | More ways to generate professional income |
| **Global by Design** | Multilingual experience; country/city discovery; international professional network | Broader reach across language and geographic boundaries |

Reputation is an **outcome across multiple pillars**, not merely a badge system. Verification, published qualifications, user reviews, and visible contributions must be represented accurately and should not be conflated with scientific proof of examination quality.

## 6. Product status and roadmap

Status labels below distinguish **current/implemented**, **in development**, and **planned**. These are based on internal product discussions as of the draft date and should be checked against the live deployment before investor distribution.

### Current / implemented or recently built

- Multilingual product experience in **English, Spanish, Ukrainian, and Russian**.
- Access-request and account-onboarding flows.
- Expert profiles and professional information, with profile visibility controls.
- Professional community with discussions, answers/comments, language filtering and content moderation controls.
- Expert directory with geographic discovery and service listings.
- Service/profile browsing, selected booking/request user flows, and reviews.
- Dashboard cards for membership usage, activity goals, level progress, service views, and order statuses.
- Membership and level mechanics in product UI/business logic, subject to commercial launch readiness.

**Do not equate an implemented interface with a fully operational paid marketplace.** Check payment processing, order fulfillment, and real-world usage separately.

### Planned / phased expansion

- Reliable end-to-end paid client marketplace, commission collection and fulfillment operations.
- Paid consultations and mentoring services among examiners.
- Facilitated roundtables, mastermind groups, and peer matching.
- Professional cases, articles, and publications with appropriate privacy/moderation review.
- Academy, structured training, educational partners, and learning offers.
- More advanced analytics, international discovery, and additional geographic markets.

### Roadmap framing

1. **Community and professional identity:** Profiles, onboarding, discussions, directory, trust foundations.
2. **Marketplace and monetization:** Orders, reviews, professional services, paid plans, commissions.
3. **Education and workflow depth:** Academy, mentorship expansion, CRM and further professional tools.

A feature's presence on the roadmap is not evidence that it has launched or that customers are paying for it.

## 7. Trust and reputation system

- Professional profile, expertise, education, certifications, and relevant practice details.
- Verification/review of submitted credentials where actually performed.
- Reviews tied to platform services where supported.
- Recognition for verified platform activity, including **Specialist** and **Master** levels based on qualifying order and review thresholds (definitions subject to finalization).
- **Founding Member** and **Industry Leader** as distinct recognition badges, not substitutes for verified transactional achievements.
- Basic level may remain invisible publicly to avoid unhelpful zero-status signaling.

**Design principle:** Differentiate *identity/qualification verification*, *platform activity*, *customer feedback*, and *community recognition*. Avoid implying that any single metric proves overall professional competence.

## 8. Business model (proposed)

### Membership

- **Free:** Entry access with limited community activity and service listings.
- **Plus:** Planned price **€50/month**, offering expanded professional capabilities and limits.
- **Pro:** Planned price **€100/month**, offering higher usage limits and advanced benefits.

Pricing, discounts, eligibility, and limits are **current proposals** and must be verified before public commitments.

### Transaction and ecosystem revenue

- Commissions on client marketplace orders, potentially differentiated by platform level and subscription.
- Commissions or fees on paid professional consultations and mentorship.
- Future learning, courses, programs, or partner educational offerings.

**Economic alignment:** As examiners derive more value and transact more successfully, Detelgy can earn recurring membership and transaction revenue. This is a business-model hypothesis, not an achieved financial result.

## 9. Geographic and language strategy

**Initial country focus:** Ukraine and Spain.

- **Ukraine:** Kyiv, Lviv, Odesa, Dnipro as initial city priorities.
- **Spain:** Alicante, Valencia, Barcelona, Madrid as initial city priorities.
- **Potential next expansions:** Poland and Mexico; further market candidates require research.

**Languages:** English, Spanish, Ukrainian, Russian. Multilingual reach is a product differentiator to test; localization alone does not establish marketplace demand.

Initial go-to-market favors **local density** and trusted professional anchors over appearing globally comprehensive from day one.

## 10. The transformation story

### Before Detelgy

A professional may depend on a relatively small referral circle, scattered online profiles, disconnected learning events, and personal contacts for guidance. Valuable expertise may remain difficult for potential customers or peers to discover.

### First value

The examiner establishes a credible professional identity, finds useful conversations or peers, and sees relevant opportunities within the platform.

### Longer-term outcomes Detelgy aims to enable

- Recognized expertise visible outside existing circles.
- Relevant peer relationships and access to specialist advice.
- Better discoverability by prospective customers.
- New client projects and, when available, paid mentoring/consulting opportunities.
- Continuing growth through learning, publishing, and contribution.

**Narrative shorthand:** *Connect → Learn → Contribute → Build Reputation → Earn → Lead.* This is a conceptual journey, **not a required linear funnel**: different professionals enter and create value through different paths.

## 11. Three paths to professional success

1. **Practitioner:** Client discovery → completed services → reviews → stronger practice.
2. **Expert contributor:** Peer discussions → cases/publications → reputation → consulting opportunities.
3. **Industry leader:** Recognition → mentorship/roundtables/training → professional influence and possible income.

Success should not be defined solely by completed marketplace orders. Some highly respected industry participants may be more active as mentors or educators than as examiners.

## 12. Proposed flywheel

**Professional expertise → Useful contributions → Discoverable reputation → Trust and connections → Relevant opportunities → Positive experiences and feedback → Further participation.**

Education improves expertise; the community makes expertise and relationships visible; the marketplace creates opportunities; reviews and publications strengthen reputation. Each connection is a **hypothesis to measure**, not a demonstrated network effect yet.

## 13. What success means and how to measure it

Possible product-level metrics:

- **Activation:** Completed professional profile; first meaningful discussion/reply; first useful connection.
- **Engagement:** Active professionals, substantive contributions, repeat participation, peer interactions.
- **Trust:** Profiles with verified credentials; credible reviews; completed service outcomes.
- **Marketplace:** Qualified inquiries, request-to-booking conversion, fulfilled orders, GMV and net revenue.
- **Professional earning:** Earnings from examinations and, later, peer consultations/mentorship.
- **Professional development:** Attendance at learning events; mentorship participation; educational completions.
- **Retention:** Professionals returning because they gain ongoing practical value.

Avoid presenting early platform traffic or UI events as proof of career improvement. Seek direct qualitative evidence of outcomes.

## 14. Evidence gaps and research priorities

Before creating a fundraising version of the deck, gather:

1. Bottom-up number of commercially active polygraph examiners in initial markets.
2. Interviews confirming priority pains, willingness to join/pay, trust expectations, and existing alternatives.
3. Customer-side evidence: search behavior, service demand, booking friction, and willingness to transact online.
4. Competitive landscape: professional associations, directories, messaging communities, local lead-generation platforms, training providers.
5. Actual activation, engagement, marketplace conversion, revenue, retention, and unit economics where available.
6. Concrete examples/testimonials, with permission and accurate attribution.

**Explicit caution:** No invented TAM figures, adoption forecasts, customer quotes, user testimonials, traction, or revenue numbers.

## 15. Presentation guidance for Codex

**Audience:** Prospective investors, strategic partners, founders and stakeholders.

**Purpose of the first deck:** Communicate the examiner-side value proposition and strategic opportunity. It is a *vision/stakeholder deck*, not yet a fully evidenced fundraising deck.

**Main character:** A capable polygraph professional whose expertise is currently less visible and connected than it could be.

**Central claim:** *Detelgy helps turn expertise into connections, reputation, and economic opportunity.*

**Tone:** Credible, human, confident but never overpromising. Lead with user pains and desired outcomes, not a feature checklist.

**Narrative structure:** Market context → Fragmentation → Professional pains → Jobs to be done → User transformation → Value pillars → Journey and flywheel → Economic opportunities → Vision.

**Design:** Premium professional identity; use actual Detelgy branding/assets where available. Keep data diagrams editable. Use conceptual illustrations only when they communicate a specific point. Distinguish product reality from future vision and source factual market statements.

**Output language:** English for slide copy and narration, with source files suitable for future localization.
