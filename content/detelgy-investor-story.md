# Detelgy — Stakeholder Vision Deck: Narrative & Slide Blueprint

**Document type:** Source-of-truth story specification for a multilingual web presentation, exports and future vision video  
**Version:** Draft 1.0 — October 15, 2026  
**Primary language:** English  
**Supported locales:** `en`, `es`, `uk`, `ru`  
**Companion documents:** `detelgy-product-brief.md`, `detelgy-icp-personas-jtbd.md`  
**Audience:** Founders, strategic partners, early supporters, Industry Leaders and prospective investors  
**Deck category:** Stakeholder / product vision deck, **not yet a validated fundraising pitch**.

## 1. One-line thesis

**Detelgy helps polygraph professionals turn expertise into meaningful connections, credible reputation, professional opportunities and potential earnings.**

### Central transformation

**From a skilled but often isolated professional → to a connected, recognized and more opportunity-rich professional.**

Narrative framework: **Expertise → Connections → Visibility → Trust → Opportunities → Professional success.**

This is a proposed mechanism, not a proven conversion or causal model. Do not imply that joining guarantees employment, clients, professional certification, improved examination accuracy or increased income.

### Core language

- Positioning: **The global professional ecosystem for polygraph examiners.**
- Core promise: **Helping polygraph professionals become more successful.**
- Brand line: **Where expertise becomes opportunity.**
- Action line: **Connect. Grow. Earn. Lead.**

These are working options, not approved final legal or brand copy.

## 2. Narrative principles

1. Lead with **the professional's lived experience**, never a feature inventory.
2. Tell a **before → friction → opportunity → transformation** story.
3. Center on the examiner (supply-side user). Buyers are a critical second side but are not the protagonist of this deck.
4. Respect three distinct paths: practitioner, knowledge contributor, and leader. They overlap and are not ranks.
5. Every depicted pain is an **unvalidated research hypothesis** unless backed by interviews or sourced evidence.
6. Distinguish **already built, in development, planned, and hypothetical**. A visual concept does not prove functionality is live.
7. Professional success has multiple dimensions: competence, connections, recognition, opportunities and income.
8. Keep claims on polygraph reliability, scientific validity, or test accuracy **out of this deck** unless independently verified and appropriately contextualized.
9. Avoid declaring a moat, network effect, market size or revenues as established facts without evidence.
10. Use concrete examples, minimal text and meaningful diagrams.

## 3. Target viewing formats

- **Primary:** 14-slide, 16:9, ~8–12 minute live stakeholder presentation.
- **Secondary:** self-guided web deck with concise presenter notes / expandable detail.
- **Exports:** selectable-language PDF first; editable PPTX later only if needed and fidelity can be maintained.
- **Future video:** 75–90 second brand vision film adapted from the same narrative, not a literal slide-to-video export.
- **Design language:** Detelgy dark navy / white / restrained blue, readable typography, thoughtful real-world portraits and precise diagrams. Use verified tokens and logo from the product repo; do not guess official hex colors.

## 4. Slide-by-slide specification

Each slide has a canonical `id`, an English working headline, a key audience takeaway, a design direction, and an evidence/claim requirement. IDs are stable across every language.

### S01 — The promise

- **Headline:** Where expertise becomes opportunity.
- **Supporting line:** Helping polygraph professionals connect, grow, build reputation and unlock professional opportunities.
- **Single takeaway:** This is about professional outcomes, not software features.
- **Visual:** Confident professional subject with a subtle connected-network motif. Minimal typography. Detelgy logotype treated as a genuine asset.
- **Status:** Vision/positioning.
- **Presenter note:** We succeed when our professionals get tangible value.

### S02 — A global profession, a fragmented journey

- **Headline:** A global profession. A fragmented ecosystem.
- **Single takeaway:** Professional identity, communication, discovery, learning and commercial services live in disconnected places.
- **Visual:** Diagram showing disconnected channels: personal sites, referrals, messaging groups, associations, seminars, social media and local directories.
- **Evidence required:** Credible references for industry associations and alternative channels. Do NOT invent total global examiner counts or TAM. Insert sourced figures only after research and attribution.
- **Presenter note:** The opportunity is to connect parts of a professional journey, not to pretend existing networks are absent.

### S03 — Who we serve first

- **Headline:** Start with professionals who want to grow.
- **Single takeaway:** Primary ICP is the independent/commercially active examiner; recognized experts and emerging professionals complement the ecosystem.
- **Visual:** Three persona columns, with the primary ICP visibly emphasized, not stereotypes or demographic claims.
- **Personas:** Growth-Oriented Examiner (primary); Established Expert / Industry Leader (trust/knowledge); Emerging Professional (learning/long-term supply).
- **Evidence:** Personas are fictional composites / working hypotheses until validated through Expert Discovery.

### S04 — Our primary persona

- **Headline:** Skilled at the work. Limited by the system around it.
- **Single takeaway:** A capable examiner can lack time, visibility or access to a larger professional network.
- **Visual:** One believable fictional examiner portrait plus concise profile cards (goals, alternatives, frustrations, desired progress).
- **Copy direction:** Focus on motivation and context, not a precise invented biography or income.
- **Evidence:** Clearly label 'illustrative persona'.

### S05 — How the professional works today

- **Headline:** Too much depends on individual effort and personal connections.
- **Single takeaway:** Professional growth currently requires stitching together multiple disconnected tools and relationships.
- **Visual:** Examiner in center with six surrounding islands: learn, discuss, ask for help, publish, gain visibility, find clients.
- **Avoid:** Claim that _all_ examiners work alone, or that associations and communities offer no value.

### S06 — The underlying pains

- **Headline:** Expertise alone does not guarantee opportunity.
- **Single takeaway:** Frictions are isolation, discoverability, trust building, learning access, client acquisition and limited monetization paths.
- **Visual:** 5–6 short pain cards with believable first-person phrasing.
- **Example quotes:** 'Who can I ask about a difficult professional situation?' / 'How can the right clients find me?' / 'How can I demonstrate my expertise outside my existing circle?'
- **Evidence:** These are _illustrative hypotheses_, not real verbatim interview quotations. Label accordingly or paraphrase without quotation marks.

### S07 — Four Jobs To Be Done

- **Headline:** Four jobs. One professional journey.
- **Single takeaway:** Become better; become known; find opportunities; monetize expertise.
- **Visual:** Four connected blocks, each anchored in a short situation → motivation → desired outcome.
- **JTBD wording:**
  1. When I encounter new challenges, help me learn from experienced peers.
  2. When I have valuable skills, help me build a credible professional reputation.
  3. When I want to grow, help me access clients, collaborators and professional opportunities.
  4. When others value my knowledge, help me offer consultations, mentorship or teaching.
- **Evidence:** Working JTBD hypotheses from the companion personas document.

### S08 — Before / With Detelgy

- **Headline:** From fragmented effort to connected professional growth.
- **Single takeaway:** Detelgy can make professional participation more discoverable, trusted and economically useful.
- **Visual:** Two-column transformation with parallel items, e.g. local-only reach → cross-border discovery, isolated knowledge → shared learning, fragmented credentials → centralized professional profile, informal visibility → publications/reviews.
- **Copy rules:** Use 'can', 'enables', 'designed to' where outcomes are not proven. Do not imply all planned features are already live.

### S09 — The platform as a growth system

- **Headline:** Different tools. One goal: professional success.
- **Single takeaway:** Features are coordinated around user progress.
- **Visual:** Four equally legible pillars around the professional:
  - **Connect:** community discussions, roundtables, mastermind conversations, peer networking.
  - **Grow:** structured learning, consultations, mentorship and eventual Academy.
  - **Build Reputation:** professional profiles, qualifications, reviews, publications, recognition.
  - **Earn:** client service discovery and bookings, peer consultations, mentorship, education opportunities.
- **Foundation:** Global and multilingual by design.
- **Product reality:** Some community/profile/discovery workflows are built; many consultation/Academy mechanisms are planned. Add an optional `status` attribute to features.

### S10 — Customer Journey

- **Headline:** The journey from joining to leading.
- **Single takeaway:** Participation leads toward several kinds of professional value, not only booked tests.
- **Visual:** Horizontal or winding progression: Discover → Join → Participate → Connect & Learn → Build Reputation → Find Opportunities → Earn / Lead → Refer & Contribute.
- **Aha Moments:** Practitioner: first qualified inquiry; Established Expert: first meaningful peer consultation request; Emerging Professional: first useful exchange with a peer or mentor.
- **Evidence:** Aha Moments are _hypotheses_ until supported by usage data/interviews.
- **Accessibility:** Never require a microscopic eight-step chain; allow two-row layout on narrow screens.

### S11 — Compounding value / flywheel

- **Headline:** Every meaningful contribution can create more value.
- **Single takeaway:** Knowledge-sharing, reputation and qualified marketplace activity may reinforce each other.
- **Visual:** Circular loop: Contribute → Become Discoverable → Build Trust → Connect / Receive Opportunities → Earn / Learn → Contribute Again.
- **Risk note:** This is a **proposed flywheel**, not a demonstrated network effect. Do not promise more orders with every contribution.

### S12 — Multiple paths to success

- **Headline:** Professional success has more than one path.
- **Single takeaway:** Professionals may build a practice, deepen their expertise, or become mentors/leaders; these paths are complementary.
- **Visual:** Three parallel paths:
  - **Practitioner:** Discoverability → Qualified inquiries → Completed work → Reviews/reputation.
  - **Expert:** Participate → Publish → Consult → Recognition.
  - **Leader:** Mentor → Teach → Contribute → Professional influence.
- **Important:** 'Industry Leader' recognition is distinct from transaction-based levels; do not assign Master level automatically by title.

### S13 — Value for the professional and business model

- **Headline:** Create value first. Monetize the value created.
- **Single takeaway:** Detelgy offers pathways to benefit for members and sustainable revenue opportunities for the platform.
- **Visual:** Two connected panels:
  - **Professional:** better discoverability, peer access, trust signals, client inquiries, paid expertise offerings.
  - **Platform:** Free / Plus / Pro memberships, commissions on completed services, future professional education and peer services.
- **Evidence:** Pricing is proposed; no invented ARPU, GMV, growth, conversion or earnings figures.
- **Optional expansion later:** Verified metrics, unit economics and realistic bottom-up market model.

### S14 — The vision / invitation

- **Headline:** The professional home for the global polygraph community.
- **Supporting line:** One professional identity. One place to connect, contribute, learn and create opportunities.
- **Closing line:** When our professionals succeed, Detelgy succeeds.
- **Visual:** Inclusive international network of professionals, restrained brand mark and a specific stakeholder invitation.
- **CTA variants:** 'Join us in building the next chapter' (partners); 'Help shape the professional ecosystem' (Industry Leaders); 'Explore the opportunity' (investors).
- **Evidence:** Future vision, not established market leadership.

## 7. Multilingual requirements (from day one)

### Locale policy

- Use **`en`, `es`, `uk`, `ru`** identifiers; Ukrainian language tag is **`uk`**, not `ua`.
- Canonical routes: `/en`, `/es`, `/uk`, `/ru` (and optionally `/en/slides/s07` etc.).
- A locale switch keeps the same slide `id` and available query/fragment state.
- Default locale `en`; detect browser preference optionally, but respect an explicit URL locale.
- Real translations must cover ALL visible slide content, chart labels, notes, alt text, UI controls and PDF export metadata.
- No per-switch AI translation call: pretranslate and editorially review four locales, then switch instantly in the browser.
- Do not use runtime machine translation for public investor copy. AI can draft translations offline, followed by human review, especially terminology around qualifications and polygraph practice.
- Allow UI to accommodate text expansion (notably German-like length is not relevant, but ES/RU/UK still vary); do not position text inside image assets.
- Use adequate Cyrillic-capable fonts and correct punctuation. Never distribute fonts without rights.

### Suggested content model

Maintain exactly the same semantic keys for every locale, e.g.:

```text
content/
  source/
    detelgy-product-brief.md
    detelgy-icp-personas-jtbd.md
    detelgy-investor-story.md
  slides/
    en.json
    es.json
    uk.json
    ru.json
  notes/
    en.json
    es.json
    uk.json
    ru.json
```

Example:

```json
{
  "locale": "en",
  "slides": {
    "s01": {
      "headline": "Where expertise becomes opportunity.",
      "description": "Helping polygraph professionals connect, grow and build reputation.",
      "imageAlt": "Professional examiner connected with peers"
    }
  }
}
```

The English file is canonical. Use a schema validator (Zod or similar) to detect missing locale keys and broken placeholders. Keep numeric values and status labels in structured nontranslatable data, not copied manually across language JSON files.

### Translation workflow

1. Founder approves the English narrative and glossary.
2. Generate editorial drafts for ES, UK and RU.
3. Review terminology with native speakers / local experts, especially professional and regulatory terms.
4. Validate translated JSON against the English schema and inspect slide overflow at 1920×1080 and small viewports.
5. Publish all four locales together, or hide locales that have not passed review.

## 8. Presentation web application architecture

**Recommended stack:** Standalone React/Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind with deterministic slide components and an optional presentation helper library if it improves keyboard navigation. Do not import the entire main Detelgy application; re-use only approved brand assets and design tokens.

### Core MVP features

- Static or server-rendered language routes, one shared slide layout.
- 14 slides with full-screen presentation mode and next/back keyboard arrows.
- Slide index / progress and clickable overview.
- Locale switcher that preserves selected slide.
- Accessible contrast, alt text and reduced-motion support.
- Responsive preview and readable mobile alternative (stack content rather than squeeze a wide slide).
- PDF export for selected locale. Prefer deterministic server-side Playwright or prebuilt/cached locale PDFs to avoid long waits or browser-dependent print results.
- Browser print CSS; stable slide/page dimensions and no cropped text.
- SEO metadata for public pages; social share preview.

### Later, not required for MVP

- Editable `.pptx` export, if a dependable conversion strategy is selected and visually tested.
- Private presenter notes and optional access controls for sensitive investor versions.
- Multiple decks (Stakeholders, Industry Leaders, Customers, Investment) sharing the same renderer.
- Analytics on aggregate presentation engagement with consent/privacy safeguards.
- Automatic visual regression checks across all slides and languages.
- Video scene generation pipeline with Remotion / FFmpeg; this is separate from Magnific image-to-video production.

### PDF/export mechanics

- `Download PDF` exports exactly the current language, not a browser auto-translation overlay.
- Suggested stable filenames: `detelgy-vision-en.pdf`, `detelgy-vision-es.pdf`, `detelgy-vision-uk.pdf`, `detelgy-vision-ru.pdf`.
- Prebuild and deploy four PDFs after a content release if that is more reliable or inexpensive than on-demand rendering.
- If server-side generation is used, cache outputs by content version and locale; protect against excessive generation load.

## 9. Domain and deployment strategy

- Project repo: `detelgy-presentations` (standalone, avoiding accidental changes to production `app.detelgy.com`).
- Production target: **`promo.detelgy.com`** deployed via a separate Vercel project.
- Add the custom domain to the Vercel project and create/adjust the DNS record using **the exact DNS target Vercel provides**. Do not assume a specific CNAME/A value without checking the dashboard.
- Connect Git repository; enable preview deploys for pull requests.
- Add redirects for `/` → `/en` or locale negotiation, and `hreflang` alternates between four routes, where applicable.
- Decide public indexing vs `noindex` for investor-only decks; never leak confidential fundraising numbers via static client bundles or public URLs.
- Prefer public vision deck on `promo.detelgy.com` and authenticated/protected distribution for future confidential investor materials.

## 11. Codex production acceptance criteria

1. Every slide corresponds to the stable IDs and outcomes in section 4.
2. English content is reviewed before auto-drafting translations.
3. All four locales use the **same layout logic**, with translated text not baked into generated images.
4. Language switching preserves the current slide and does not request live model translation.
5. Exported four PDF versions have all 14 slides in correct order with fonts embedded/available and no clipping.
6. Product status and planned features are labeled accurately.
7. No unsupported quantitative facts or testimonial quotes appear.
8. Run automated screenshots (desktop 16:9 + narrow viewport) and manually inspect for overflow, misalignment and contrast.
9. Use Detelgy's actual logo, brand assets and provided UI screenshots when available; placeholders must remain clearly marked.
10. Deploy to a preview URL first; production custom domain is configured only after content and translation review.
